# Group Susu — Implementation Plan

## Context

Group susu (rotating savings) **is the product.** Users create susu groups, contribute weekly, and
rotate receiving the full pot. There is no second feature line.

A *susu* is the oldest financial institution in West Africa: a circle contributes a fixed amount each
week, and each member takes the whole pot in turn. Nobody outside Ghana can build this without
understanding how it actually works, and no platform has shipped it.

## Compliance Gate — read before writing any payment code

Ghana's **Payment Systems and Services Act, 2019 (Act 987) §4(1)** prohibits any body corporate from
operating a payment system or providing a payment service without a Bank of Ghana licence. §4(2)(m)
lists **"provision of any electronic platform for payment or receipt of funds"** as a licensed
activity. §9 makes unlicensed operation a criminal offence.

**An earlier draft of this plan specified escrow — "money held in system." That is the licence
boundary. It has been removed.**

**Design rule: never take custody of the pot.** Money moves member → mobile money → member. We keep
the *record*, not the funds. Every credible competitor in Ghana and Nigeria publishes the same
sentence — *"we never hold your money"* — and it is free legal risk reduction.

## Decisions Resolved

| Decision | Choice |
|----------|--------|
| Group creation | User-created (not automated) |
| Rotation | Fixed order |
| Frequency | Weekly |
| Miss policy | 1 free skip (turn skipped, pays next round + missed share), removed after 2 misses |
| Platform | App only. **No USSD, no savings pockets, no split-bill.** See master roadmap |
| **Custody** | **Never hold funds. Route collection and payout through a licensed partner. The app is the ledger, not the bank** |
| Payout | Triggered by the licensed rail, not by a button press. Admin cannot release a payout manually |
| Admin role | Administrative only (manage members, verify, flag, restart). **No custody, no withdrawal privileges** |
| Trust model | Server-side verification of every contribution against the rail. Admin accountable for flagging, never debited from a balance we do not hold |
| Contribution amounts | Variable per member (admin sets target, members pay what they can, total must hit target) |
| Cycle restart | Admin decides (group ends after all rounds, admin can refresh/restart) |
| Verification | Server-side, against the payment rail. Never member-typed text, never admin-only |
| Member pricing | **Free, always. No fee on contributions, ever** |

## Monetization Constraints

Free for members, forever. The payer is never the person whose trust we need.

| Tier | Who | Price |
|------|-----|-------|
| Free | Members, admins with up to 5 groups | GH¢0 — no card, no trial clock |
| Collector | Admin running 10–50 groups | GH¢90 / 170 / 350 per month |
| Institution | Credit unions, SACCOs, MFIs, market associations | GH¢1,500 – 8,000 per month |
| Diaspora | Someone abroad funding a group's round | 1.5% of the amount funded |

**Rules we do not break:**
- **Members never pay.** Not a trial, not a small fee. At a 1% take rate the whole national market
  is worth GH¢2.53 per group per month, and the Bank of Ghana suspended a telco's 0.75% transfer
  fee in May 2026 "to protect consumers."
- **No fee on a contribution.** Members are depositing; a fee there reads as a tax on thrift.
- **Admins are free until they run 10+ groups.** A trader with 2 groups is not a business.
- **Keep our name off the contribution line.** If members believe the app takes their money, the
  circle dies.
- **No ads, ever.** Not worth one institutional customer, and it withdraws the trust we sell.

**The money is the Institution tier:** audit trail, PAR / ageing buckets, individual member
statements, BoG-category formatting. 25 paying institutions ≈ breakeven.

Decisions taken during implementation:

- Rounds advance either by a confirmed payout or by an admin closing the round early.
  A closed round below target is recorded as a `skipped` payout and moves the turn on
  without paying anyone.
- `memberShare` is the even pot share (pot / active members) and is what a member is
  charged when they miss, rounded to two decimals.
- Admin trust starts at 100. Each flagged transaction costs 25 and blocks the round;
  the second flag demotes the admin to a regular member.

## Data Model

**Superseded.** The sketch below is the original app-side design and does not match what was
built. It uses `Decimal` money, a `reference_number` field, a `flagged` status on the
contribution, and no ledger at all. The real model is in `backend/susu/models.py` and
`ledger.py`, and differs on every point that matters for money:

| Original sketch | Shipped | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `DecimalField` amounts | `BigIntegerField` pesewas | Floats cannot represent pesewas; decimal in JSON reintroduces the float |
| `SusuContribution` flat | `Contribution` with FK to `Membership` | A member holds a seat per group; contributions attach to the seat |
| Status `flagged` | `flagged`/`disputed` are round-level blocking states | A dispute blocks the round, not one row |
| No ledger | Append-only `LedgerEntry` + `LedgerPosting` | Conservation is checkable, not asserted |
| `payout_status` on round | `closing_pesewas`, `receiver_index`, no payout | No custody, no payout trigger |
| — | `IdempotencyKey` on contribution | An offline replay must not double-post |
| — | `manual_verification_reason` | A human decision stays distinguishable from a provider's |

Original sketch, retained for reference only:

```python
# susu/models.py
class SusuGroup(models.Model):
    name = models.CharField(max_length=100)
    description = models.TextField(blank=True)
    target_amount = models.DecimalField(max_digits=10, decimal_places=2)  # pot size per round
    total_rounds = models.PositiveIntegerField()  # number of rounds (typically = member count)
    current_round = models.PositiveIntegerField(default=1)
    current_turn_index = models.PositiveIntegerField(default=0)  # index in rotation
    is_active = models.BooleanField(default=True)
    created_by = models.ForeignKey(User, related_name='created_susus')
    created_at = models.DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True)
    updated_at = models.DateTimeField(auto_now=True)

    @property
    def pot_size(self):
        return self.target_amount  # fixed per round

    @property
    def is_complete(self):
        return self.current_round > self.total_rounds

class SusuMember(models.Model):
    group = models.ForeignKey(SusuGroup, related_name='members', on_delete=models.CASCADE)
    user = models.ForeignKey(User, related_name='susu_memberships', on_delete=models.CASCADE)
    order = models.PositiveIntegerField()  # position in rotation
    is_active = models.BooleanField(default=True)  # removed after 2 misses
    joined_at = models.DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True)

    class Meta:
        unique_together = ['group', 'user']
        ordering = ['order']

class SusuContribution(models.Model):
    member = models.ForeignKey(SusuMember, related_name='contributions', on_delete=models.CASCADE)
    round_number = models.PositiveIntegerField()
    amount = models.DecimalField(max_digits=10, decimal_places=2)
    mobile_money_provider = models.CharField(max_length=20, choices=[('mtn_momo', 'MTN MoMo'), ('vodafone_cash', 'Vodafone Cash')])
    reference_number = models.CharField(max_length=100)  # mobile money transaction ref
    status = models.CharField(max_length=20, default='pending', choices=[
        ('pending', 'Pending Verification'),
        ('verified', 'Verified'),
        ('failed', 'Failed'),
        ('flagged', 'Flagged for Review'),
    ])
    paid_at = models.DateTimeField(null=True, blank=True)
    verified_at = models.DateTimeField(null=True, blank=True)

class SusuRound(models.Model):
    group = models.ForeignKey(SusuGroup, related_name='rounds', on_delete=models.CASCADE)
    round_number = models.PositiveIntegerField()
    receiver = models.ForeignKey(SusuMember, related_name='received_rounds', on_delete=models.CASCADE)
    payout_amount = models.DecimalField(max_digits=10, decimal_places=2)
    payout_status = models.CharField(max_length=20, default='pending', choices=[
        ('pending', 'Pending - contributions not verified'),
        ('processing', 'Processing'),
        ('completed', 'Completed'),
        ('failed', 'Failed'),
    ])
    payout_reference = models.CharField(max_length=100, null=True, blank=True)
    completed_at = models.DateTimeField(null=True, blank=True)
```

## Backend Changes

### New API Endpoints

### Actual Endpoints (`backend/growl/urls.py`)

These differ from the table above, which described an earlier design. The important differences:
verification is per **contribution** rather than per group, and there is **no payout endpoint**.

| Endpoint | Method | Auth | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------------|
| `/api/auth/token/` | POST | — | Issue JWT access + refresh pair |
| `/api/auth/token/refresh/` | POST | refresh | Rotate refresh token, reuse detection revokes the family |
| `/api/groups/` | GET / POST | JWT | List the caller's groups; create a group (creator is seated as admin, invite code returned) |
| `/api/groups/join/` | POST | JWT | Join by invite code |
| `/api/groups/<id>/rounds/current/` | GET | member | Current round: roster, frozen shares, pot, ledger residual |
| `/api/groups/<id>/audit/` | GET | member | Per-member-visible audit feed |
| `/api/rounds/<id>/` | GET | member | Round detail |
| `/api/rounds/<id>/contributions/` | POST | member | Log a payment (amount, provider, reference, idempotency key) |
| `/api/contributions/<pk>/<transition>/` | POST | varies | `void` (payer, own only), `verify` (admin, never own), `reverse` (admin, never own) |

**No payout endpoint exists, by design.** No member and no admin can reach a payout instruction
through this surface; the pot is released by the licensed rail, not by a request. P-S1, P-S7.

There is deliberately no `fail` transition: a member asserting their own payment failed could walk
away from cedi they actually sent. That belongs to the verification worker, which does not exist
yet. Until it does, `verify` is the documented manual fallback, not the primary path.

### Contribution Flow

```python
# POST /api/rounds/{id}/contributions/
Request:
{
    "amountPesewas": 10000,      # integer pesewas; never a float, never client-trusted
    "provider": "hubtel",
    "reference": "ACME-0001",    # provider transaction reference, unique per round
    "idempotencyKey": "..."      # replays return the original result, never a second payment
}

Flow:
1. Refuse a non-member, a non-integer amount, or an amount below the floor (400).
2. Refuse a second open attempt while one is held — the freeze rule. (400)
3. Refuse a duplicate reference for that member. (409)
4. Refuse a reused idempotency key belonging to a different member's payment. (409)
5. Create the contribution as `pending` and post nothing to the ledger.

A logged payment is a **claim, not a cedi.** It moves the pot only when `verify` posts the
collection entry, inside the same transaction as the status change — so a member can never be
told their payment verified while the money record disagrees.
```

### Payout Flow

**There is no payout endpoint and no payout trigger in the app.** Payout is out of scope by
compliance decision, not by omission. The pot is released by the licensed rail, and Growl keeps
the record rather than the funds. Round completeness is computed and exposed; nothing in this
codebase moves money out.

### Verification Auto-Check

**Not implemented.** A worker should poll pending contributions against the provider, confirm
them from the provider's response (not from a request), and mark them failed when the provider
denies them. Until that exists, `verify` is the manual admin fallback, and the absence of `fail`
is deliberate: see the endpoint table above.

### Admin Liability Logic

The app holds no funds, so there is no balance to debit. Accountability is **reputational and
procedural**, not financial — which is also the only version we can legally operate.

```python
# When a contribution cannot be verified, or a member disputes a verified one:
# 1. Mark contribution as 'flagged'
# 2. Block payout for that round until it is resolved
# 3. Record the flag against the admin who verified it
# 4. Admin trust score decreases
# 5. After 2 flagged transactions → admin loses admin privileges
# 6. Group votes for a new admin, or disbands
# 7. The dispute record is permanent and visible to every member
```

**Why not debit the admin:** we hold no money, so there is nothing to debit, and taking a payment
from a member would be an unlicensed payment service. The leverage is visibility and removal, not
clawback. This is also the design that survives a member dispute without us becoming a party to it.

## Key Business Rules

| Rule | Enforcement |
|------|------------|
| Admin sets target amount | Defined at group creation |
| Members pay variable amounts toward target | Each contribution is logged individually |
- Full pot to the receiver | No payout endpoint exists. The rail releases the pot; Growl records it |
| **Shortfall blocks the round** | The round does not advance on a verified total below target, and the missed shares are booked as debt |
| **No manual release** | There is no payout route for an admin, and no button that moves money. Not an omission — a compliance decision |
| Minimum contribution | GH¢1 (100 pesewas), enforced by a database constraint |
| Max 50 members per susu | Prevents unmanageable groups |
| Unique member per group | No duplicate memberships |
| **Never hold funds** | No escrow, no custody, no float. Collection and payout route through a licensed partner |
- **Optimistic UI may show `pending`, never `verified`** | The client may not self-assert verification. Only a server transition or the provider worker may do that |

## Security

- JWT auth on all endpoints; short-lived access tokens, refresh rotation with reuse detection
- **Split transition authority, by design** (`backend/susu/api.py:525`):
  - `void` — the member's own unsettled attempt, and only their own. Cancelling something you
    started needs no authority beyond starting it. Posts nothing, because nothing arrived.
  - `verify` — admin only, and never on the admin's own contribution. An admin who can verify
    their own payment is the one case where the check approves itself.
  - `reverse` — admin only, and never their own. Reversing your own payment is the withdrawal a
    group cannot see.
  - **No `fail` transition exists.** Marking a payment failed asserts the provider never
    confirmed money, and a member who could say that about their own payment could walk away from
    cedi they actually sent.
- **No payout endpoint.** No member and no admin can reach a payout instruction. P-S1, P-S7.
- Admin accountability: trust score, permanent dispute record. **No financial clawback** — we
  hold no funds, so there is nothing to debit, and debiting a member would itself be an
  unlicensed payment service
- Idempotency key on every contribution write, so an offline replay cannot double-post
- Reference uniqueness per member per round, refused with 409
- All amounts are integer pesewas, server-validated; the client is never trusted with money math
- `select_for_update()` on round state changes

## Error Handling

| Scenario | Behavior |
|----------|----------|
| Fake/unverifiable reference | Contribution stays `pending`; it never counts toward the pot |
| Member disputes a verified payment | Round marked `disputed`, blocking; dispute is permanent and member-visible |
| Reversal (money came back out) | `reverse` posts the exact mirror; the original entry stays, never edited |
| Member cancels an attempt they never sent | `void` releases their slot and posts nothing — nothing ever arrived |
| Member misses week | Turn skipped, owes missed share + current week |
| Member misses 2nd week | Removed from rotation |
| Admin wants to restart | Requires confirm. Resets rounds/turns |
| Target not reached | Round does not advance on a verified total below target |
| Duplicate contribution | Refuse — one open attempt per member per round (freeze rule) |
| Duplicate reference | Refuse — 409, the same cedi cannot be claimed twice |
| Replayed idempotency key | Return the original result; never a second payment |
| Member declares their own payment failed | **No such transition exists.** A member who could say this could walk away from cedi they sent |

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Operating without a BoG licence (Act 987) | **No money moves until the legal opinion and licensed-partner agreement are signed.** No custody, no escrow, no float |
| Admin runs off with the pot | **We never hold funds.** Collection and payout route through a licensed partner, there is no balance to steal, and the app has no payout button. Rail reconciliation + member-visible ledger + trust score |
| Fake mobile money references | Reference uniqueness, and verification from the provider's response once the worker exists. Manual `verify` is the interim fallback |
| Concurrent writes on round state | `select_for_update()`, plus a unique verified contribution per member per round |
| Complex UI overwhelms users | Progressive disclosure; simple cards → detail on tap |
| Low adoption of susu tab | Onboarding tour; highlight during group creation |

## Open Questions

1. **Mobile money API verification — worker implemented, pending PSP credentials.** Hubtel EPSP is primary and Fincra the fallback; both are licensed PSPs, so reference verification arrives *with* the Phase 0 partner agreement rather than as a separate integration. Until then `verify` is manual. **Never** wire PawaPay, Eversend, or Kollekt as primary.

2. **Sanctions/PEP screening provider.** World-Check, Dow Jones, or BoG public list? The `COMPLIANCE_SCREENER` setting supports stub/world_check/dow_jones; contract one before launch.

3. **Payout fee treatment.** Whether rail transfer fees come out of the pot must be stated plainly in the member-facing copy, not buried in terms.

4. **Miss policy edge case (D9).** Resolved: a removed member's debt is never silently cleared. `Membership.deactivate` requires an explicit `forgive_debt` flag. With `forgive_debt=True` the debt is written off as a balanced group-level `REVERSAL` posting (DEBIT member_payable / CREDIT debt_receivable, `round=None`) and `debt_pesewas` is zeroed — the write-off is preserved in the audit trail and the closed round's `conservation_residual`/`ledger_residual_pesewas` stay zero. With `forgive_debt=False` the debt is preserved on the membership and survives departure, counting against any future payout. Covered by `susu/tests/test_full_cycle.py::TestMemberRemovalAndDebtDisposition` (3 tests).

5. **Retention / anonymisation.** NOT a todo for this effort — already implemented and tested under `compliance/retention.py` + `compliance/management/commands/purge_expired_personal_data.py` (+ `compliance/tests/test_privacy.py`). The real schedule is 7-year statutory hold on ledger/audit (Act 1044 s.38), 2-year inactive-account anonymisation (Act 843 s.24), 3-year consent/requests, 7-day tokens — pinned to the published notice by `test_retention_schedule_in_the_notice_matches_the_enforced_one`. No "30-day ER window" exists; do not invent one.
