Shareable links or QR codes: After you log an expense, the app can generate a payment request link. You send it via WhatsApp, email, or SMS, and the other person pays without needing an account.

💳 Direct payment integration: The app could connect to mobile money, PayPal, or bank transfers. You record the split, then trigger a direct payment to the person’s account. They don’t need the app — just their usual payment method.

📝 Manual settlement tracking: If they won’t use digital payments, you can mark the expense as “settled offline” once they hand you cash or transfer outside the app. The app still keeps the record for your group balance.

🔄 Invite flow: Some apps encourage onboarding by letting you send an invite when you split with a non‑user. They can join to see the breakdown, but it’s optional.
